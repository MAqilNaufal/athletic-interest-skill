# Troubleshooting editorial drafts

## It feels like a slide deck

**Signals:** headline-plus-panel layouts repeat; every section enters with the same card; visual language does not change with the claim.

**Fix:** replace the sequence concept, not just the styling. Assign a different evidentiary job: document for attribution, chart for measurement, diagram for mechanism, footage for lived consequence.

## The visual repeats the narration

**Signals:** VO says the same sentence shown as large text; B-roll only echoes nouns.

**Fix:** make the visual establish proof, relation, scale, or consequence that the voice alone cannot provide. Reduce redundant text to labels, units, or source context.

## Motion feels dead

**Signals:** objects drift by a few pixels; camera moves without anticipation or result; consecutive frames are effectively identical.

**Fix:** define an entry state, meaningful operation, and result. Use a deliberate hold or cut when no operation is needed.

## Mechanism is unclear

**Signals:** arrows, coins, product images, or icons move but no unit, constraint, or recipient is named.

**Fix:** name the actors, resources, units, relationship, and constraint. Show a before/after state that matches the claim.

## Source material looks authoritative but proves little

**Signals:** generic photographs or source crops are used as evidence for broad assertions.

**Fix:** narrow the claim to what the asset proves; add the underlying data/document; label illustration and reconstruction distinctly.

## It matches a reference superficially but still feels wrong

**Signals:** similar palette or typography, but different editorial jobs, visual medium, or scene logic.

**Fix:** compare a continuous reference sequence with the candidate by question, evidence, operation, and consequence. Rebuild the wrong grammar before polishing style.

## Render is complete but approval is impossible

**Signals:** no source ledger, no direct audio review, unclear whether visuals are draft or final.

**Fix:** mark missing gates `UNREVIEWED` or `BLOCKED`; do not infer approval from export success.
