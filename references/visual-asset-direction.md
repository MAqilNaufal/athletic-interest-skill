# Visual asset direction

## Start from the visual job, not a search query

Every selected asset must fill one named job: proof, context, mechanism, comparison, consequence, or reset. Record it in `visual-asset-plan.csv` before downloading or building the shot.

## Selection order

1. **Primary evidence:** filings, data tables, court records, official video, contemporaneous reporting, interviews, or original footage.
2. **Licensed context:** editorial photography, archival material, locations, and generic cutaways that establish setting but do not prove a claim.
3. **Original explanation:** charts, diagrams, maps, animated objects, and recreations used to explain a disclosed mechanism.
4. **Generated illustration:** only as labelled context or reconstruction; never present it as documentary proof.

Do not use a generic trophy, office, product image, map, dashboard, or stock crowd merely because the narration names it. If it cannot establish the statement, assign it context or replace it with evidence.

## Per-beat visual plan

For every narration cue, choose one primary asset class and specify:

- what the viewer must newly understand;
- whether the asset proves, contextualizes, compares, or explains;
- source and rights/usage status;
- crop, treatment, labels, source-credit, and screen time;
- the visible operation: isolate, compare, transfer, accumulate, replace, reveal, or hold;
- fallback if the preferred asset is unavailable.

A sequence may layer assets, but the evidence asset remains legible long enough to be read. Decorative texture must not conceal a document, unit, label, or chart axis.

## Asset treatment rules

- Preserve the meaning of a source crop; do not crop away the qualifier that changes the claim.
- Make source cards brief but readable. Attribution does not substitute for showing the relevant evidence.
- Use stills with deliberate camera or compositional change only when that change has an editorial reason.
- Charts retain dates, units, scope, and stable axes during comparisons.
- Diagrams visibly distinguish measured quantities from conceptual relationships.
- Reconstructions and generated visuals are visibly marked as illustration when viewers could mistake them for reportage.

## Approval condition

A visual plan is ready only when every sequence has a primary visual job, a recorded source/status, and a disclosed fallback. Missing exact assets remain `BLOCKED`; do not silently replace them with generic vectors.
