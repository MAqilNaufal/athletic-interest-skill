# Audiovisual review limits

Media ingestion, metadata extraction, frame sampling, captions, automated transcription, waveform analysis, and model descriptions provide different kinds of evidence. None alone certifies continuous visual review or direct listening.

## Minimum record

For a reviewed source, record identity, duration, frame rate where relevant, inspected intervals, review method, and any audio-review intervals separately. Preserve source hashes only when they are lawful and useful for reproducibility; do not commit copyrighted media or full transcripts.

## Model-assisted analysis

Treat model-assisted audio or video observations as provisional unless the method establishes what the model actually received and the result is independently checked. Use controls where appropriate, including silent input and overlapping segments. A failure in a control invalidates the affected claim rather than proving a source characteristic.

## Approval boundary

Do not claim full audiovisual coverage until continuous visual and direct audio review cover the whole source without gaps. When those capabilities are unavailable, report the narrower result plainly.
